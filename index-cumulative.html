Interactive map. Countries are shaded by total participants through the selected year; darker means more. Use the year slider at the bottom left to change the year. Use the "Participant Data" checkbox to show a circle for each country, sized by the number of writers; circles can be reached with Tab, opened with Enter and closed with Escape. With the map focused, arrow keys pan and plus or minus zoom. The same information is available as a table below the map.
